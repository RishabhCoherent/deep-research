# Senior Analyst Must-Cover Checklist

Every research brief MUST address the items flagged must-have for the detected intent.
Gap Analyzer: add a `gap_fill` question for every missing must-have row below.

---

## Shared (all intents — always required)
- Market size (with geography and time frame)            [category: size]
- Key drivers (top 3, quantified where possible)        [category: drivers]
- Key constraints / risks (top 3)                       [category: constraints]
- Recent news / events (last 90 days)                   [category: macro]

---

## market_sizing
must-have:
- Total market size with segmentation by product/chemistry/application  [category: segmentation]
- Growth rate (CAGR or YoY)                                              [category: size]
- Regional split (at least 3 regions)                                    [category: geography]
- $/unit or price benchmark (USD per unit)                               [category: size]
- Demand outlook 3–5 year horizon                                        [category: outlook]

---

## competitive
must-have:
- Top 5–10 players by market share                     [category: competitive]
- Share trend over last 2 years                        [category: competitive]
- Recent M&A / market entry / exit activity            [category: competitive]
- Pricing behaviour (leader vs follower dynamics)      [category: competitive]
- Regional competitive breakdown                       [category: geography]
- Segmentation by player type                          [category: segmentation]

---

## regulatory
must-have:
- Primary regulator(s) and jurisdiction                [category: regulatory]
- Recent regulation changes (last 24 months)           [category: regulatory]
- Pipeline regulation (next 12 months)                 [category: regulatory]
- Compliance cost / market impact estimates            [category: constraints]
- Cross-border / trade policy implications             [category: macro]

---

## trend
must-have:
- Top 3 emerging trends with adoption metrics          [category: drivers]
- Technology or behavioural shift enabling trend       [category: technology]
- Timeline / adoption curve (early / mainstream / late)[category: outlook]
- Counter-trends or constraints limiting adoption      [category: constraints]
- Geographic variation in trend adoption               [category: geography]

---

## technology
must-have:
- Current technology readiness level (TRL)             [category: technology]
- Key players / IP holders in the technology           [category: competitive]
- Cost trajectory / learning curve                     [category: size]
- Commercial deployment timeline                       [category: outlook]
- Substitute / competing technologies                  [category: substitution]

---

## geographic
must-have:
- Market size comparison across ≥3 regions             [category: geography]
- Regional growth rate differential                    [category: geography]
- Policy / regulatory differences driving divergence   [category: regulatory]
- Supply-chain or infrastructure differences           [category: value_chain]
- Regional competitive landscape variation             [category: competitive]
